Linen & Oak Bedroom Bedroom design in dark oak and linen

Space
Bedroom
Materials
Dark oak, linen, travertine
Type
Private residence
Status
Design visualization

A bedroom in dark, soft tones: a dark-oak wall that curves at its ends to wrap the bed, a linen headboard, travertine side tables and slender pendants hanging from the ceiling.

Light comes in through the curtain and stays soft. The pendants replace bedside lamps, leaving the side tables free for a book and a candle.

Afternoon sun falls in a wedge of light on the wall above the dark wood line, softened by the sheer curtain.

The materials

  • Dark oak

    A deep tone that gives a room weight and balances pale surfaces.

  • Linen

    A soft fabric with a slightly rough texture that softens the wood and stone around it.

  • Travertine

    A natural stone with small pores that cast soft shadows in side light.

Material names describe the look as it appears in our visualizations. We choose the exact stone type, finish and supplier for each home together with you.
