II

Oak & Stone Living Living room design with oak shelving and a stone plinth

Space
Living room
Materials
Oak, veined stone, plaster, dark oak
Type
Private residence
Status
Design visualization

The living room is built around one wall: a television set flush into plaster, slender oak shelving that holds only a few chosen pieces, and a long stone plinth running the length of the wall to tie them together.

The shelves are spaced unevenly, so each object has its own place: a ceramic piece, a book, a candle. Facing the wall, a dark-oak coffee table with rounded corners balances the pale tones.

LIGHT

Light

A soft line of light beneath the oak paneling separates it from the stone, so the paneling appears to hover above the plinth.

The materials

  • Light oak

    Straight grain and a pale tone that reflects plenty of light. It suits large joinery without weighing the room down.

  • Veined stone

    Cool to the touch, with natural veining. It holds its own without extra decoration around it.

  • Dark oak

    A deep tone that gives a room weight and balances pale surfaces.

Material names describe the look as it appears in our visualizations. We choose the exact stone type, finish and supplier for each home together with you.
