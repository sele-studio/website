SERVICES

Interior design services, from planning to styling

Every project starts by listening: how you live at home, what works there today and what doesn’t. From there we move to planning the space, design and materials, support through the build and the final styling, step by step, in the same clean, precise and right language. The studio works across central and southern Israel.

How the process works

Five steps, one home

  1. 1

    Discovery

    A first meeting, listening to needs and dreams, and shaping a realistic budget and timeline.

  2. 2

    Concept

    A design direction, mood boards and materials: the language the home will be built from.

  3. 3

    Detailed design

    Working drawings, joinery, lighting and electrics. Every detail settled before we begin.

  4. 4

    Build & guidance

    Close guidance through the build, coordinating trades and checking the quality of finish.

  5. 5

    Handover & styling

    Final styling, the lights come on, and your home is ready for life.

What to bring to the first meeting

You don’t have to come prepared. But if you have them, it helps to bring:

  • A floor plan, if you have one
  • Photos of the space as it is
  • Inspiration you loved — screenshots are fine
  • What matters to you at home, and what isn’t working today
  • A budget range and timeline, if you already have them
