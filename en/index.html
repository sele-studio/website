SERVICES

What we do

  • Interior design

    A complete design language for the home: materials, joinery, furniture, lighting and textiles, from concept to the last detail.

  • Space planning

    Layout, circulation, storage and light, with orderly working drawings that trades can build from.

  • Kitchen design

    Island, work zones, floor-to-ceiling joinery, stone and integrated light, planned around how you live.

  • Bathroom design

    Layout, stone, taps and concealed systems, waterproofing, light and storage: a small room that needs precise decisions.

  • Renovation support

    Present through the build: coordinating suppliers and trades, site visits and checking details and finish.

  • Styling & finishing touches

    The final layer that turns a house into a place: objects, art, greenery and precise styling. The small details that leave a feeling.

MATERIALS

Materials you want to touch

Few materials, used generously: stone, wood and textile. This is how they look in our work.

  1. Veined stone

    Cool to the hand, quiet to the eye. The veining is the only ornament the room needs.

    In the Stone Kitchen

  2. Light oak

    The wood that holds the morning light. Straight grain, a quiet tone.

    In Oak & Stone Living

  3. Travertine and walnut

    A stone with memory: every pore is a small shadow. Beneath it, the warm depth of walnut.

    In the Travertine Bath

  4. Linen and dark oak

    Soft, slightly rough, breathing. And beside it, an anchor: the tone that gives a room weight and calm.

    In the bedroom

Material names describe the look as it appears in our visualizations. The exact choice for each home — stone type, finish and supplier — we make together with you.

PROCESS

Five steps, one home

  1. 1

    Discovery

    A first meeting, listening to needs and dreams, and shaping a realistic budget and timeline.

  2. 2

    Concept

    A design direction, mood boards and materials: the language the home will be built from.

  3. 3

    Detailed design

    Working drawings, joinery, lighting and electrics. Every detail settled before we begin.

  4. 4

    Build & guidance

    Close guidance through the build, coordinating trades and checking the quality of finish.

  5. 5

    Handover & styling

    Final styling, the lights come on, and your home is ready for life.
